# VR-TUTORIAL – Sprachtexte (English voice-over)

Ein Text pro Seite. Die fertige Aufnahme bekommt den Namen der Seiten-ID, z. B. `01_hands.ogg` (oder .mp3 / .wav), und kommt nach `client/tutorial/` (oder `user://tutorial/`). Sie startet dann automatisch, sobald die Seite aufgeht, und der Knopf PLAY AUDIO spielt sie noch einmal ab.

Die Texte gelten für beide Händigkeiten. Sie sagen „pointer hand“ bzw. „card hand“ statt links/rechts und nennen beide Tasten („A or X“), denn welche gilt, hängt von der Handwahl auf Seite 1 ab. Alle Texte sind etwa 10–25 Sekunden lang.

---

### 01_hands – Welcome to the VR Tutorial!
Hi, I'm Jimbo! Welcome to the VR Tutorial. I'll show you the most important moves, one at a time, and each page only goes on once you've done the move yourself. First things first: which hand should point the laser beam, and which one holds your cards? Point at your choice and pull the trigger. You can switch hands later in the VR menu.

### 02_select – Select cards
Here are your cards, in your card hand. Point the beam of your other hand at a card and pull the trigger to select it. Pull again to unselect it. Go on, select at least two cards.

### 03_move_card – Move a card in your hand
You can put your cards in any order you like. Point at a card and hold the trigger, and the card lifts out of the fan. Move the beam onto another card and let go there. Your turn: move a card to a new place.

### 04_flick – Play: the flick
Now let's play them, the fun way. Bring your pointer hand close to the selected cards and hold the grip to grab them. Then toss them onto the table with a quick little flick of the wrist, and let go. Give it a try!

### 05_play_button – Play: the button
In a hurry? Select your cards and press the lower face button, A or X, on your pointer controller. The cards are played at once, no grabbing needed. Try it now.

### 06_shoulder – Discard: over your shoulder
Cards you don't want? Throw them over your shoulder! Select them, grab them with the grip, swing them back and let go behind your head. Go on, nobody's watching.

### 07_discard_box – Discard: the box
If you like it tidier, use the box. Select cards, grab them and hold them over the Sell and Discard box on the table. Let go of the grip, and they drop right in.

### 08_discard_button – Discard: the button
And the quick way again: select cards and press the upper face button, B or Y, on your pointer controller. Discarded!

### 09_sort_stick – Sort: thumbstick or grip
Let's tidy up your hand. On the controller that holds your cards, either click the thumbstick down, or squeeze the grip while nothing else is in reach. Each press sorts your cards by rank or by suit. Pick whichever you like!

### 10_sort_turn – Sort: turn your hand
There's one more way to sort. Turn your card hand on its side, as if you were showing your cards to someone next to you. A blue Sort button pops up above the cards. Point at it and pull the trigger.

### 11_deck_info – Your deck
Wondering what's still left in your deck? Just hold one of your hands over the deck on the table. Its cards show up for as long as your hand stays there. Try it!

### 12_shop_grab – Shop: grab it
Welcome to the shop! Everything stands on the sales counter. Reach out, grab the joker with either hand, pull it towards you and let go. That's it, it's yours! Pointing with the beam works too, but this time, use your hand.

### 13_reroll – Shop: reroll
Don't like what's on offer? The reroll machine next to the counter brings new things, for a few dollars. Grab the ball on top of its lever and pull it down, just like a slot machine.

### 14_pack_tear – Booster pack: rip it open
A booster pack! Grab it with both hands, then pull your hands apart and rip it open. Don't be shy!

### 15_pack_laser – Booster pack: the laser
Here's another pack. This time, just point your beam at it and pull the trigger. It opens straight away.

### 16_use_throw – Use a card: throw it up
Tarot, planet and spectral cards wait in the card stand on the table. Grab the planet card with your pointer hand and throw it up into the sky. Let go above your head!

### 17_use_hand – Use a card: onto your hand
Every card also works like this: take The Hermit with your pointer hand and hold it right at your card hand. When it glows gold, let go.

### 18_wheel – The Wheel of Fortune
Ooh, the Wheel of Fortune! Grab its rim with the trigger or the grip and swing it round, or just give it a swipe with your hand. If it lands on WIN, one of your jokers gets an edition. Spin it!

### 19_joker_order – Jokers: from left to right
Just like in Balatro, your jokers count from left to right, so their order really matters. Grab a joker, carry it and put it down at another place. Your turn: sort them into the order three, one, two, from left to right.

### 20_joker_sell – Jokers: sell one
Don't need a joker any more? Grab it and let go of it right over the Sell and Discard box. It drops into the slot, and you get its sell value back.

### 21_adjust – Adjust your VR layout
You can put everything at the table wherever you like. Press the menu button on your card hand's controller and choose Adjust VR Layout. Every item gets a yellow ball. Grab the score tower's ball, move it, and while you hold it, push the stick up or down to change its size. Don't worry, it's just practice: your own layout comes back afterwards.

### 22_backgrounds – Three backgrounds
You can play in three different places. Green is for passthrough, so you can see your real room. Dark is calm and easy on the eyes. And then there's the Casino. Open the VR menu and press Background to switch. Try all three, then keep your favourite.

### 23_options – Good to know: the options
Good to know: in the VR menu you can make things calmer or simpler. Calm Lights turns off the flickering lights. Casino Sounds turns the sound effects on or off. Hands shows or hides the virtual hands. And Jimbo sends me away. Don't worry, I won't be offended! Press Got It to carry on.

### 24_achievement – Achievements and rewards
Some moves only VR lets you do, and they unlock achievements. Each one has a reward that changes how VR looks. Press Unlock for your very first one! Rewards can be switched on and off, too. Open the VR menu, choose Achievements, and switch the reward off to finish this page.

### 25_done – You're ready!
That's all the moves! You can play this tutorial again any time with the VR Tutorial sign on the menu table. Have fun at the table, and Safe Travels!
